Using MarginForge

Choose an engine, a marketplace, and Single Product or Bulk Analysis.

Selling Price Engine

Enter product costs and your target margin. The recommended selling price updates automatically after you finish typing. Negative target margins are supported.

Breakeven Selling Price is the lowest supported price with a non-negative profit, using the original .05 / .09 ending pattern.

Margin Engine

Enter the exact current selling price and costs to see profit, margin, and fees. Price rounding is visible but disabled in this engine.

Choose the market

Amazon supports United Kingdom, France, and Germany. eBay, OnCommerce, Shopify, and TikTok use UK calculations. Amounts must be entered in the selected currency; switching countries does not convert them.

Enter costs correctly

Enter costs excluding recoverable VAT. For Amazon UK, select the category and enter the FBA fee. France applies its configured FBA increase above €12; Germany keeps the entered fee unchanged.

For eBay, OnCommerce, and Shopify, unit cost is multiplied by quantity and shipping is deducted once per order. Enter the total order or pack selling price. On eBay, select Top Rated or Below Standard; Below Standard includes the additional 6% fee.

TikTok UK

Enter product cost, TikTok shipping fee and seller shipping separately. Smart Promotion defaults to 5%. Commission is 9% plus a separate 1% fee; each percentage fee is rounded to two decimals. The supplied spreadsheet has no VAT deduction. Margin uses selling price.

Price rounding

Choose Next penny / cent, Round up to .99, or Original endings when finding a selling price. The recommended price meets or exceeds your target margin. Breakeven always uses Original endings.

Bulk Analysis

  1. Select the engine, marketplace, and country.
  2. Download its matching template. Keep the headers and replace the example rows.
  3. Find-price templates contain costs; current-margin templates also require SellingPrice.
  4. Upload your CSV, or paste its contents or Excel tab-separated data.
  5. Set the batch target margin if applicable, then click Run Analysis.
  6. Review row errors and click Export Results to download the CSV.

Up to 2,000 products per batch. Re-run analysis after changing batch data or settings. EAN / ASIN / SKU identifies each product.

Read the results

The cost bar shows profit, fees, fulfilment, product costs, and VAT as shares of the selling price. eBay, OnCommerce, and Shopify margin uses revenue excluding VAT; Amazon margin uses selling price including VAT, following the agreed calculations.

For a loss, the bar shows proportions of total deductions and a separate loss notice. Expand Calculation details for formula notes.

Marketplace Comparison

Use UK to compare channels at a selling price, based on costs from the single-product form. Use individual calculators when shipping or other inputs differ between marketplaces.
